🎓 Engineering & Viva Defense Cheatsheet
Q: How does MeshPay work without internet?
Sender locks payment data using the Bank's public key (RSA-OAEP + AES-GCM) and transmits it over Bluetooth Low Energy. The packet hops phone-to-phone via gossip routing until any device gets 4G signal and uploads it to the bank.
Q: Can intermediate stranger phones tamper with amount or steal the PIN?
No. Intermediate nodes only carry opaque ciphertext. Tampering with any bit fails AES-256-GCM authentication tag verification. Only the Central Bank's private key can decrypt the envelope.
Q: How is double-spending and duplicate delivery prevented?
Each packet has a SHA-256 hash. The bank checks its idempotency cache upon receipt; duplicate packets uploaded by multiple phones are discarded immediately without decrypting or double-charging.
