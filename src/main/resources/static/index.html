🎤 2-Minute Viva Cheatsheet (Easy Explanations)
Q: What is this project in simple words?
Jab dono phones me internet nahi hota (jaise basement me), tab payment phone-to-phone Bluetooth ke through hop karti hai aur jab koi stranger bahar 4G me aata hai, wo bank ko deliver kar deta hai.
Q: Can intermediate strangers steal money or change the amount?
No. The payment note is locked with the Central Bank's RSA public key and AES-256-GCM. Strangers only see opaque ciphertext. Any bit-flip fails authentication.
Q: What if 3 different phones upload the same packet?
The bank hashes the ciphertext (SHA-256). The first copy settles; copies 2 and 3 are dropped immediately as duplicates without charging twice.
Q: How do you prevent offline double-spending?
Offline spending is capped at ₹2,000 (like RBI UPI Lite). When reconnecting, the bank settles on a first-come-first-served basis.
